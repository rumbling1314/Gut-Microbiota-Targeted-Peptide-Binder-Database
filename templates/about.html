Gut Microbiota Targeted Peptide Design Database

A Bioinformatics Resource for Identifying Functional Bacteria in the Complex Gut Microbiota

An open Flask + SQLite resource that integrates surface-target identification, computational peptide (binder) design and molecular-dynamics validation for gut bacteria, organized in three linked layers: Species → Target_Protein → Binder.
